Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026 • Effective Immediately

At FastMatch, your privacy, personal boundaries, and safety are our foundational values. This Privacy Policy details how we handle, process, and protect your personal information when you use our mobile applications and services.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Details: Name, phone number, email address, age, gender, and login credentials (including OAuth tokens from Google or Apple).
  • Profile Media: Profile photos, bio, preferences, and interests you choose to display.
  • Live Audio & Video Streams: Camera and microphone streams are transmitted in real-time via peer-to-peer WebRTC connections. We do not record or save your private live video chats.
  • Messages & Interactions: Encrypted text messages, friend requests, favorites, and matches.
  • Transaction Data: Virtual coin purchases and subscription status processed securely via Google Play In-App Billing or Apple App Store. We do not store your credit card details.
  • Approximate Location: Country or general region used to facilitate global discovery and match filtering.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your data solely to provide, operate, and enhance your FastMatch experience:

  • To authenticate your identity and protect against fraudulent accounts.
  • To match you with compatible people based on your discovery preferences.
  • To process real-time audio/video calls and chat messaging.
  • To provide customer support and ticket assistance.
  • To ensure community safety through user blocking, reporting, and moderation.

3. Data Protection & Security

All data transmitted between your device and FastMatch servers is encrypted using industry-standard TLS/HTTPS and Secure WebSockets (WSS). We employ strict access controls and server-side encryption to protect your records.

4. Account Deletion & Data Rights

In compliance with international privacy regulations (including GDPR and CCPA) and Google Play Developer Policies, you hold full ownership of your data:

  • In-App Deletion: You can permanently delete your account and all associated data anytime in the app via Settings > Delete Account.
  • Web Request: You can submit a deletion request online at our Account Deletion Page or email support@fastmatch.app.
