Child Safety Standards Policy

Developer: FastMatch Team • App: FastMatch • Effective Date: October 2026

Zero Tolerance Policy: FastMatch maintains an absolute zero-tolerance policy against Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE) and Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM). Any violation results in immediate, permanent account termination and referral to law enforcement.

At FastMatch, developed and operated by the FastMatch Team, we are deeply committed to protecting children and ensuring a safe online ecosystem. In compliance with Google Play's Child Safety Standards policy and international child protection laws, we enforce comprehensive safeguards across our platform.

1. Strict Age Restriction (18+ Only)

FastMatch is exclusively designed for consenting adults aged 18 years and older. Minors (individuals under the age of 18) are strictly prohibited from registering an account, accessing, or using FastMatch.

  • Age verification is required during account registration and profile setup.
  • Accounts found to belong to minors, or profiles displaying images depicting minors, are immediately permanently banned and removed from our servers.
  • FastMatch enables Google Play's age-gating mechanisms to restrict declared minors from downloading the app.

2. Prohibition of Child Sexual Abuse & Exploitation (CSAE)

We strictly prohibit any of the following activities on or through FastMatch:

  • Uploading, transmitting, possessing, or distributing Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM).
  • Grooming, soliciting, exploiting, or attempting to communicate with minors for sexual or inappropriate purposes.
  • Sharing, facilitating, or linking to child exploitation material or services.
  • Depicting, encouraging, or facilitating sexual exploitation, abuse, or violence against children.

3. In-App Reporting & User Feedback Mechanism

FastMatch provides robust, easily accessible in-app mechanisms empowering users to report violations instantly:

  • User Profile Reporting: Tapping the report button on any user profile enables users to flag inappropriate content or suspected underage accounts.
  • In-Chat Reporting & Blocking: Inside every direct conversation, users can block and report users with immediate effect.
  • Support Tickets: Users can submit detailed safety reports directly via the in-app Help & Support center.

4. Content Moderation & Enforcement

  • All reports relating to child safety are escalated with the highest priority and reviewed 24/7 by our safety team.
  • Confirmed CSAM content is immediately and irreversibly purged from our servers.
  • Offending accounts are permanently banned with device and identity blacklisting to prevent re-registration.

5. Mandatory Law Enforcement & NCMEC Reporting

In accordance with federal and international reporting mandates (including 18 U.S.C. § 2258A), FastMatch immediately reports all known or suspected instances of Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) and exploitation to the National Center for Missing and Exploited Children (NCMEC) and cooperates fully with global law enforcement authorities.

6. Dedicated Child Safety Point of Contact

FastMatch has designated a specialized safety and compliance team responsible for child safety oversight and handling notifications from Google Play, child protection organizations, and regulatory authorities.

Contact Our Safety Team

For urgent child safety concerns, reports, or legal inquiries, please contact our designated team directly:

  • Designated Child Safety Email: safety@fastmatch.app
  • General Support Email: support@fastmatch.app
  • Response Time: Child safety reports are reviewed within 24 hours.
