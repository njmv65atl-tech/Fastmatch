Account & Data Deletion Request

At FastMatch (operated by FastMatch Team), your privacy and data ownership are our foundational values. You have the full right to delete your FastMatch account and all associated personal data at any time.

Method 1: Direct In-App Deletion (Instant)

You can permanently delete your account directly inside the FastMatch mobile app:

  1. Open the FastMatch app on your device.
  2. Log in to your account.
  3. Navigate to Settings (user profile tab).
  4. Scroll down to the bottom and tap Delete Account.
  5. Confirm your request. Your account, profile, chats, and records will be deleted immediately.

Method 2: Request Deletion via Email (Web Request)

If you have uninstalled the app or cannot log in, you can submit an account deletion request via email:

  • Send an email from your registered email address or provide your registered phone number to our support team:
  • Email: support@fastmatch.app
  • Subject: Account Deletion Request - FastMatch
  • Include your registered Phone Number, Email, or User ID in the message body.

Data That Will Be Deleted

  • Profile Data: Display name, age, gender, biography, country, and interests.
  • Photos & Media: All uploaded profile photos and gallery images.
  • Communication History: Chat messages, call history, and match records.
  • Social Connections: Friends list, connection requests, and favorites.
  • Device Identifiers: Push notification tokens and session data.

Retention & Processing Time

In-app deletions take effect immediately. Email requests are verified and processed by our security team within 48 to 72 hours (and no later than 30 days in compliance with GDPR and Google Play Developer Policies).
